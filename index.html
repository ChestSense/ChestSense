B2

Five chests.
Every match.

Every scan resolves five chests across the island. Each one gets a tier and a find chance, so you know which one is worth the detour.

  1. Common 52–71%
  2. Rare 61–80%
  3. Epic 69–88%
  4. Legendary 77–95%
  5. Pirate 83–99% The only chest that can hold a Pirate Shop token.
D4

Four steps.
One drop.

  1. 01

    Enter your key

    Open ChestSense and type the 10-character key you got in the Discord. It locks to your PC the first time you use it.

  2. 02

    Launch Fortnite

    ChestSense waits in the background. When the game starts it attaches on its own and tells you when it's ready.

  3. 03

    Scan on the bus

    Press Insert while you're on the Battle Bus and hit Scan. Five chests show up on the map, each with its find chance.

  4. 04

    Drop and loot

    Click where you're landing to get the chests around that spot. A small radar on the side of your screen keeps them in view.

Questions

How do I get a key?

Keys are handed out in the Discord. Join, ask, and you'll get your own 10-character key.

The menu doesn't show up in game.

Set Fortnite to Windowed Fullscreen (Settings, Display, Window Mode). Insert only opens the menu while Fortnite is running.

Can I use my key on two PCs?

No. A key locks to the first PC it's used on. Using it on a second PC gets it refused there.

Do I type the key every time?

Yes, every time you open ChestSense.

Does it work on console or mobile?

No. ChestSense runs on Windows PCs only.
